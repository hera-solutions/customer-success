The story is not that customers are leaving. It is that a third of our revenue comes from customers who are not using what they pay for, and that is the renewal risk nobody was watching.

What we learned about the business

Our revenue grows when our customers hire

Billing is $9 per active associate per month, charged daily and invoiced a month behind. Associate count explains 96% of what a customer pays.

That means when a DSP adds drivers, our revenue rises automatically. It is why existing accounts grew 13.9% with nobody selling to them.

The flip side. We have almost no pricing lever inside an account. Only 4 of 249 customers pay any flat fee. Our revenue is a pass-through on a headcount base we do not control.

The only way to raise revenue per associate is to sell something not priced per associate. HeraAi is on 13% of accounts. That is the one real expansion lever we have.

What I need from you

Five decisions

  1. Replace the retention target. We are at 95% addressable. I suggest 96 to 97%, or moving the goal to the trial funnel where there is real room.
  2. Resolve the three unexplained billing accounts. About $17,000 a year. I need to know whether they are intentional before I touch them.

  5. Decide on the legacy pricing migration. Nine customers are still on $5 to $7 module pricing. Moving them to $9 is fair but "cost savings" is already 10% of our churn, so it should be sequenced deliberately.
