# CS Health Weekly

**As of 2026-08-03.** 241 paying tenants, 234 customers, **$181,623 per month**, 22,067 active associates.

**All figures are monthly revenue.** Hera bills month to month with no contract, so a customer can leave at the end of any month. Annualising would imply a commitment that does not exist. Multiply by 12 for a yearly view if needed.

Engagement window 14 days, roster window 30 days, revenue direction over the last 4 closed invoices. Billing is $9 per active associate per month.

Generated by `analysis/tenant-engagement/classify.py`. Model rationale: `analysis/cs-health-baseline-2026-07/findings.md`.

## How to read this

Plain-language version of every term used below.

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | What that customer or group pays us every month. Not a yearly figure |
| **Associate** | A driver. We charge $9 per active associate per month, worked out daily |
| **Active** | Only associates the customer has marked Active are billed. The customer controls that, and they often update it late |
| **Staffed roster** | A day's driver schedule with at least one route actually assigned to a person. An empty schedule does not count |
| **Human message** | A message somebody at the customer actually sent. Automatic per-driver deliveries do not count |
| **Scorecard** | The weekly Amazon performance file. Every working DSP should upload one a week |
| **Dark** | We can see no sign of a person using the product |
| **Declining** | Their active associate count has fallen more than 5% across their last four invoices, so our revenue from them is falling |
| **Roster cleanup** | The customer deleted a batch of old driver records on one day. Their bill drops, but they did not lose drivers. This is good hygiene, not churn |

**The two questions this report answers separately.** *Will they stay at all* is about whether anyone is using the product. *How much will they pay* is about whether their driver count is rising or falling. They are not the same thing: a customer can use the product daily while their business shrinks, or grow fast while ignoring us entirely. Mixing them into one score hid both problems, so they are measured apart.

### Paused: inventory and document signing

**Neither is measurable from here, and neither has a usable entitlement flag either.** Both features live in the Athena app, which has no table in this DynamoDB account, so there is no usage to count.

The two flags on `Tenant` that look like they would help do not:

- `featureEnabledInventoryManagement` and `featureAccessInventoryManagement` are both `true` on 949 of 954 tenant rows, including long-churned ones. That is a global default switched on for everybody, not a record of who bought it, so a count off this field would mean nothing.
- Document signing has **no field at all** on `Tenant`. It is too new to have been added.

So measuring either one needs RDS access or the Athena API. Paused by decision on 2026-07-30. Both are intended to become scored signals later, once adoption is high enough that absence is the exception.

## Caveats

- Monthly figures come from the last **closed** invoice. The current month is still accruing as `Pending` and must never be used for revenue.
- Thresholds were set from the July 2026 distribution and are **not seasonally adjusted**. Peak season is not yet accounted for, so a summer decline cannot be separated from a seasonal trough.
- Revenue direction uses associate counts, which the customer controls via Active status and updates late. Declines are checked for bulk cleanups; the check is heuristic, not certain.
- `NOT_ENTITLED` tenants lack the rostering module and are scored only on coaching and scorecard activity.
